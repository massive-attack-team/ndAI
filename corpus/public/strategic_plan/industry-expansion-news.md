# Trade Press: Vantable Opens Enterprise Sales Division

Vantable, a screening-infrastructure vendor competing in the same
high-throughput oncology tooling space, announced this week that it has hired
a dedicated enterprise sales lead and is standing up a separate sales
motion for large pharma accounts, distinct from its existing self-serve
subscription product.

Industry analysts note this is a common move for lab-software vendors once
they have a handful of large accounts large enough to need dedicated support
and custom contracts, rather than a self-serve signup flow.

The announcement did not include headcount numbers, timeline, or reporting
structure - those specifics were not disclosed.
