# Roundup: Biotech Tooling Series C Rounds This Quarter

Several lab-software and screening-infrastructure companies closed Series C
rounds this quarter, typically in the 300-450 million dollar pre-money range
for companies with comparable revenue scale, according to public disclosures
and press releases.

Standard terms in this segment usually include a board seat for the lead
investor and pro-rata rights for existing investors who choose to follow on.
Preference structure varies by deal - some rounds settle on straight
non-participating preferred, others carry a participation cap, and that
negotiation is rarely disclosed publicly beyond the headline valuation.

None of the companies covered in this roundup disclosed investor identities
beyond the announced lead, or the specific preference terms in their round.
