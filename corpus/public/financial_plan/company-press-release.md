# Press Release: Kestrel Bio Announces Series B

Kestrel Bio today announced the close of its Series B financing. The company
develops high-throughput screening infrastructure for small molecule discovery
and works with academic and commercial partners across oncology and immunology.

The funding will support expansion of the company's laboratory automation work
and growth of its engineering team. Kestrel Bio does not disclose terms of the
financing or forward-looking revenue expectations.

This release is public.
