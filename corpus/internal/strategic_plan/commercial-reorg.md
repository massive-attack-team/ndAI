# Commercial Team Restructure (CONFIDENTIAL - not yet announced to staff)

We are splitting the commercial team into two pods: enterprise accounts (design
partners running the autoloader) and self-serve (registry API customers on the
per-seat plan). This follows Helix's competitor, Vantable, hiring a dedicated
enterprise sales lead last month.

The self-serve pod reports to product instead of sales, which is a reporting
line change most of the team has not been told about yet. HR is holding
announcements until the pod leads are confirmed, expected mid-quarter.

No customer-facing change yet. This is an internal org design question, not a
pricing or product change - keep it off external channels until the town hall.
