# How We Run Quarterly Planning (Internal)

Each quarter, engineering, product and commercial submit priorities two weeks
before the planning offsite. Leads rank their own team's asks, then the
offsite resolves conflicts across teams.

Anything that ships to design partners or affects pricing gets flagged for a
separate confidentiality review before it goes in the shared roadmap doc -
that review is a distinct step from ordinary planning and has its own sign-off.

This process itself isn't secret - most of the company already knows roughly
how planning works. What's confidential is whatever specific commitments come
out of it in a given quarter, not the cadence.
