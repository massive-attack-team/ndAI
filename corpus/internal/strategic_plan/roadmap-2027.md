# Unreleased Roadmap - FY27 (CONFIDENTIAL)

Q1. Ship the cartridge autoloader to three design partners only. No public
announcement. Success is defined as 500 unattended plate runs without a jam.

Q2. Open the compound registry API to external collaborators. This is the first
time customer data crosses our boundary and it requires the tenancy rework that
has slipped twice already.

Q3. Retire the legacy curve fitting service. Every customer still on the old
endpoint must be migrated first; two large accounts have not started.

Q4. Announce the autoloader publicly and begin general availability pricing at a
per-plate rate rather than the current per-seat licence. The pricing change is the
commercially sensitive part and must not leak before the announcement.
