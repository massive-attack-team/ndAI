# Project Nightjar - Board Memo (RESTRICTED)

Recommendation: acquire Helix Assay Systems for a headline figure of 384 million
dollars, structured as 60 percent cash and 40 percent stock, with 45 million held
in escrow against the outstanding patent dispute.

Rationale. Helix owns the microfluidic cartridge line that our own hardware team
has failed to reproduce in two attempts. Building it ourselves costs an estimated
three years and carries a real chance we never ship. Buying it removes a supplier
dependency that currently gates our entire high-throughput roadmap.

Timeline. Exclusivity expires on the 14th. Diligence closes two weeks after. We
intend to sign before the quarterly earnings call so the announcement lands with
the results rather than as a separate disclosure.

Risks. Helix carries an unresolved dispute with a former contractor over cartridge
tooling. Counsel rates our exposure as moderate. Retention of the Helix hardware
leads is the real risk: if the three named engineers leave, we have bought a
patent portfolio and a factory, not a capability.

This memo is restricted to the board and the deal team. Do not forward.
