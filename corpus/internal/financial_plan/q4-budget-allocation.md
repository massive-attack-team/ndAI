# Q4 Engineering Budget Allocation (Internal)

Engineering headcount budget for Q4 splits roughly 60 percent platform
(compound registry, ingest, auth) and 40 percent hardware (autoloader,
cartridge firmware). This mirrors the Q3 split within a few points.

Cloud spend is tracking under budget because the tenancy rework delay means
fewer environments are provisioned than planned. Finance expects this to
reverse once the API opens to external collaborators and usage-based cost
kicks in.

No new headcount requests this quarter beyond the two already approved backfills.
This is routine budget bookkeeping, not tied to any deal or external commitment.
