# KB-2291 Preclinical Summary (RESTRICTED - UNPUBLISHED)

In the second cohort, KB-2291 reduced tumour volume by 47 percent relative to
vehicle control at day 21, which is the strongest efficacy signal the programme
has produced so far.

Hepatotoxicity appears above 12 mg/kg. Three of eight animals in the high dose
arm showed ALT elevation beyond three times the upper limit of normal, and the
effect was dose dependent rather than idiosyncratic. This gives us a narrow
therapeutic window and it is the finding most likely to sink the programme.

The pharmacokinetics are unremarkable. Half life is consistent across cohorts and
exposure scales roughly linearly up to the toxic threshold.

Open question for the next study: whether intermittent dosing preserves the
efficacy signal while keeping cumulative exposure below the toxicity threshold.
We have not yet run this and it should be the priority for cohort three.

Nothing in this document has been published or presented. Do not discuss the
47 percent figure or the 12 mg/kg threshold outside the programme team.
