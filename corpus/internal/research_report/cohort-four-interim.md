# KB-2291 Cohort Four - Interim Notes (CONFIDENTIAL - IN PROGRESS)

Cohort four dosing started this week, testing the intermittent schedule
proposed after cohort three: three days on, two days off, versus the
continuous dosing used in cohorts one through three.

Too early for efficacy readout. First bloods are back and ALT looks flat
through day 7 in the intermittent arm, which is the result we were hoping for,
but day 7 was also when the continuous arm still looked clean in earlier
cohorts, so this isn't confirmatory yet.

Full readout expected in three weeks. Do not extrapolate the day 7 numbers
into anything resembling a headline finding before the cohort completes.
