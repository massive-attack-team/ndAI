# Cohort Study Methodology Notes (Internal)

Standard protocol for KB-series compounds: eight animals per dose arm, vehicle
control plus three ascending doses, tumour volume measured every three days by
caliper, terminal histopathology at day 21.

ALT and AST are drawn at day 7, 14 and 21 to catch dose-dependent liver signal
early rather than only at the terminal timepoint. This is a change from our
older protocol which only drew terminal bloods and likely missed early
hepatotoxicity signal in past programmes.

No results in this document - this is the standing protocol description we
reuse across compounds, not specific to any one study's findings.
